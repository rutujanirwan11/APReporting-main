## Description

Applicants and Residents, under certain law, have the right to request what personal identifiable information (PII) is stored  and they can also request it to be removed. This includes things like name, date of birth, social security number, gender, etc. These requests are submitted by the applicant or resident to the property. The property then reviews those request and sends a request to Entrata to gather the PII data, or remove the PII data
