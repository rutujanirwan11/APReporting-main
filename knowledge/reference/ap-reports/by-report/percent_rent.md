## Description

This report will display the sales amount posted by the tenant for a certain period alongside the threshold amount, percent rent rate, and the amount posted to the ledger. The period lengths can be altered to fit tenant and owner needs.  This report will also display the sales amounts for current and prior periods, and for multiple properties.
