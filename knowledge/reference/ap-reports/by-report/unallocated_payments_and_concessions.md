## Description

The Unallocated Payments and Concessions report allows you to view details for all open, unallocated resident payments and concessions when an open charge also exists on the resident ledger. This information notifies you of any instances where payments and concessions may not be appropriately allocated, thus helping maintain clean, accurate resident ledgers.
