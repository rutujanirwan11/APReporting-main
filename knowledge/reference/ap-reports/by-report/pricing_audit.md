## Description

This usage of the Pricing Audit report varies depending on what view you're looking at: The Variance view shows what the leasing agents are actually getting for the unit. When you advertise a specific price it does not necessarily mean you're getting that price for the unit. This view will show you what the units are actually leasing for. The Acceptance view gives you an inside view on what the Entrata Pricing tool is suggesting the price be for specific units and what is actually being posted. This view shows the frequency of price changes from what the tool recommends. Not only is this view beneficial for your use, but it also allows for Entrata to see if any settings need to be adjusted in order to get the prices more to what you are wanting.
