## Description

The Gross Potential Rent (GPR) - Cash Basis report is used to determine the maximum rent that a property could receive based off of market rent prices if all of their units were rented. This report shows that number as well as what is preventing the property from reaching that number. (e.g. vacancy).
The Cash Basis version of this report breaks out how the system calculates the GPR journal entry when it is set to Cash.  It includes cash receipts and change in delinquency for the period.
