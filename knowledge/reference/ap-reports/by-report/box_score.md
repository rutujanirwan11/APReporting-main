## Description

The Box Score provides key property performance data points pertaining to unit availability, lease activity, lead traffic, and make readies. Please note that when tying the report numbers out to other reports the Lead Activity and Lead Conversion datasets are based on activity performed in the period. The report excludes Other Income profiles.
