## Description

The business purpose of this report is different from what view the client is looking at. The variance view is to know what the leasing agents are actually getting for the unit. When they advertise a specific price it does not necessarily mean they are getting that price for the unit. This view of the report will give them a view on what the units are actually leasing for. The acceptance view will give the client an insight view on what we are suggesting the price be for specific units and what is actually being posted. This will give an insight view on how much prices are being changed from what we recommend. Not only is this a view for the property and company, but for Entrata to see if any settings need to be adjusted in order to get the prices more to what the clients want.
