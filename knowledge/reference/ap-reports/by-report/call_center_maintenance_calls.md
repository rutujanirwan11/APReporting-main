## Description

The Call Center Maintenance Calls report shows the count of maintenance related calls received by the leasing center. The report has two sections. The Call Center Maintenance Calls section displays a count by type; maintenance calls, maintenance emergency calls, work orders. The Calls by Hour section displays all calls received by hour of the day and includes a total call count.
