## Description

The Income Statement, also known as a Profit and Loss Statement or simply a P&L, is a financial statement that shows revenue and expenses over a specified period of time. The Income Statement helps companies measure their financial performance over the given period by outlining the revenues and expenses and calculating a net income or loss.
