## Description

This report assists student properties in tracking their renewal process along with any incentives that have been set up within renewal tiers. The non-consolidated version of the report enables properties to track residents who have accepted renewals and assisting those students who have not yet accepted in showing what their renewal rate and incentive will be based on available tiers. The consolidated version shows a high-level overview of each tier showing average renewal rates and cap information.
