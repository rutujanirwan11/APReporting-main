## Description

The Balance Sheet report is a financial statement used by investors and owners to help them understand what their company owns, what their company owes, and what has been invested. The Balance Sheet lists a company's assets, liabilities, and equity at a particular point in time, detailing the balance of income and expenditure over the preceding period.
