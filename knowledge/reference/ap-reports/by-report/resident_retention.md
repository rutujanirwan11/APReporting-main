## Description

The Resident Retention Report allows you to track and manage expiring leases. It uses a period filter to pull in all leases with a lease end date in the period selected and will display those leases in 6 categories based on the projected (for future months) or historical (for prior months) next step. Those six categories are early move-outs, NTV, Renewals, Renewal Transfers, Pending, and MTM. Common uses of this report are to follow-up on Pending Residents and to reward staff based on retention percentages. This report also has a detailed view showing one resident per line.
