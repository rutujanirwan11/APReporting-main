## Description

The Saved Payment Methods report contains datasets to display residents who have saved payment, or refund, methods, as well as those who do not. Resident and payment method details are available for view for those residents who have saved payment methods. Resident details are available for display for those who do not. This report can be used to view saved payment method details, as well as whether or not the payment method has been verified.
