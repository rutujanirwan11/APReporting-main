## Description

The Management Fees report gives you information on the management fee costs a property has to pay, and how that fee is calculated. Note for Percent of Revenue users: If adjustments to revenue are made after the fee has been generated, the Total Revenue and Management Fee Due on the report will not match the amounts on the Management Fee Invoice.
